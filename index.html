About AWS NHU

MORE THAN A COMMUNITY.
WE ARE BUILDERS.

AWS NHU is a student-driven technology community at Nazeer Hussain University, bringing together passionate students who want to explore cloud computing, artificial intelligence, software development, and emerging technologies.

Who We Are

A student-driven community at Nazeer Hussain University where passionate minds come together to explore cloud computing, artificial intelligence, software development, and emerging technologies.

Our Mission

To empower students with practical cloud knowledge, industry-relevant skills, and opportunities to innovate through AWS technologies.

Our Vision

To develop a strong ecosystem of student innovators who build impactful solutions and contribute to the future of technology.
